> Part of the **pdf2md CJK/annotated-PDF** work order — see the tracking issue for shared context, the corrections in §0, and the additive-corruption principle these cards rest on.

**Confidence:** High (defect), Medium (statistic choice). **Owns:** `crates/kopitiam-document/src/validation/{mod.rs,report.rs}`

**Depends on F-1** (shared files).

## Problem

The glyph doubling in F-3 is invisible to recovery — both sides inflate equally. Per §2.4, an absolute statistic is needed.

## Change

Add a cheap, language-agnostic statistic over the **rendered** side: rate of adjacent repeated tokens. Two candidates — pick one and justify:

- **Repeated-bigram rate** — fraction of positions where `text[i..i+2] == text[i+2..i+4]`. Catches `会話会話` and, space-tolerant, `会 会話 話`.
- **Repeated-word rate** — fraction of whitespace-delimited tokens equal to their predecessor. Catches `スーパーで スーパーで`, **misses** the interleaved CJK form.

The bigram form is more general; the observed interleaving (`会 会話 話`, not `会話 会話`) is exactly what makes the word-level check insufficient.

**Warn, do not fail.** Legitimate repetition exists (`々`, `いろいろ`, tables of identical cells). This is a signal to look, not a gate.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Synthetic fixture reproducing `会 会話 話 1: 先生が 会話を はじめます。` → warning raised, naming the page.
- [ ] Negative fixture with legitimate repetition (`いろいろな人が来ました。`, a 3-row table with a repeated cell value) → no warning.
- [ ] Real corpus: `730209` (18 occurrences) warns; a clean Latin PDF does not.
