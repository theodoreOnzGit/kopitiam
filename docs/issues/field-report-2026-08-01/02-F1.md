## Problem

`729152` emits runs of `���` and scores `recovery_ratio: 1.0`, `passes: true`.

U+FFFD is a legitimate graceful fallback at the font layer (`crates/kopitiam-pdf/src/mupdf/font.rs:554`) — a CID→Unicode lookup miss, not a crash. But by the time a document is **rendered**, a page dense in replacement characters is a failed page, and nothing says so.

## Change

Compute replacement-character density per page (count of U+FFFD ÷ content chars). Surface in `ConversionReport` per page and document-wide.

Gate: any nonzero count emits a warning; above a threshold (suggest 1% of a page's content chars, tunable) the page fails and the document does not `PASS`.

Per §2.4 this must be an **absolute** property of the rendered side — do **not** implement as an extracted-vs-rendered comparison, which cancels out.
